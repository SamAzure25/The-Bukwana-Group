WHO WE ARE

A company built around your needs.

The Bukwana Group TPY LTD is a diversified supply and services company focused on providing practical solutions to our clients.

We supply and deliver a wide variety of products, including computer equipment, stationery, cleaning materials, personal protective equipment, perishable goods and general goods.

Beyond supply and procurement, we provide professional services across logistics and transportation, cleaning, grass cutting, accommodation and ICT.

Our growing service offering also includes business consulting, marketing, financial services and cybersecurity, allowing us to support clients across multiple areas through one trusted partner.
